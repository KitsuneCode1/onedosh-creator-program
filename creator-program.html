Still have questions?

Do I need a minimum follower count? ▼
No minimum. We work with creators of all sizes. Credibility and trust matter more than follower count.
What if my audience is outside Nigeria? ▼
Perfect. We work with creators anywhere in the world. Our users can transact globally, and so can your audience.
When do I start earning? ▼
After you sign, activate, and your first user completes their first USD transaction. Then you earn $0.50/month per active user.
What's an "active user"? ▼
A user you referred who completes at least 5 USD transactions per month. You earn for every month they stay active.
Is there a cap on earnings? ▼
No cap. $0.50 per active user, per month, at every tier. Scale as big as you want.
What happens after I sign? ▼
We schedule a quick 15-minute activation call to generate your creator code, set up dashboard access, and brief you on next steps.
Can I promote on all platforms? ▼
Yes. TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Twitter, Facebook — all good. Your code works everywhere.
Still have more questions? ▼
Email creators@onedosh.com and we'll respond within 24 hours. No question too small.
